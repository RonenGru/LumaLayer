📱 Step 2: WiFi Configuration

After installation, the device will reboot. For the easiest setup, use your Smartphone:

  1. On your phone, scan for WiFi and connect to "LumaMeter_Setup".
  2. Your phone should automatically show a "Sign in to network" notification. Tap it.
  3. If it doesn't open, open your phone's browser and go to 192.168.4.1
  4. Select your home WiFi, enter your password, and hit Save.

Windows users: Disable "Connect Automatically" if your PC refuses to stay connected to the setup hotspot.
